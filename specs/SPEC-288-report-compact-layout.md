# SPEC-288 — Daily Reports: the compact executive report

Status: implemented (2026-09-22)
Depends on: SPEC-045 (the v3 executive report this re-lays), SPEC-106 (top styles ·
same-store), SPEC-107 (weekly/monthly rollup), SPEC-108 (PDF export), SPEC-231/233
(the panel · row · strip vocabulary), SPEC-242 (✦ Explain on KPI tiles)

## Problem

Owner, 2026-09-22, on `/reports`: "I want a very professional look of this report —
very compact, not so much empty space." Today the report is eleven full-width
sections stacked with `space-y-5`: two rows of three 96px KPI cards under two
section rules, a Store performance table alone across 1,600px with four rows, then
two-up cards each with 16px padding and 15px text. At 1440px the daily report is
~2,900px tall and most of every row is white.

## The rule

**Presentation changes. Behaviour does not.** Every figure, section, drill-down link
(`drilldownHref`, new tab), Explain target, PDF export, regenerate button, the
weekly/monthly note, the legacy-viewer fallback for pre-SPEC-027 payloads and the
"no report" empty state survive. `lib/report-sections.ts`, `types/reports.ts`, the
queries and the page are untouched. The scoped (per-location) report keeps its own
view (SPEC-033) — out of scope here.

## Layout (the Brief's and the Dashboard's vocabulary, SPEC-231/233)

```
date · generated by · badge                              [PDF] [Regenerate]
SUMMARY · AI ANALYSIS      one panel, the narrative
KPI STRIP (6 tiles)  Gross revenue Δ · Net revenue · Product margin · Orders Δ · AOV Δ · Refunds
┌ STORE PERFORMANCE   table ─────────┐ ┌ UNFULFILLED ORDERS BY LOCATION  table ┐  (2-up ≥ lg)
┌ TOP 10 STYLES  10 rows ────────────┐ ┌ SAME-STORE SALES  table ──────────────┐  (2-up ≥ lg)
│                                    │ ┌ AGED DEAD STOCK  buckets + top five ──┐
┌ SALES BY CHANNEL ─┐ ┌ RISKS ──────────────┐ ┌ OPPORTUNITIES ───────────┐  (3-up ≥ lg)
┌ RECOMMENDED ACTIONS  full width ────────────────────────────────────────┐
```

Rows are paired by height: the two location tables have the same row count; ten
style rows stand as tall as the same-store table over the dead-stock panel; the
three short panels share a row; the actions close the report at full width. Three
five-column tables side by side clip at 1440px (measured), so the tables stay two-up.
**Top products is shown only when the payload has no Top 10 styles** — both are
`product_performance` and the five were the first five of the ten, so the old page
listed the same rows twice.

- **Panels, not cards.** Every section is a `Panel` (compact uppercase header, hairline
  body) — the same component the Brief and the Dashboard use, so the three pages read
  as one product. Gaps are 12px (`gap-3`), never 20.
- **One KPI strip** replaces the Financials and Operations rows: six tiles from a pure
  builder, `lib/report-strip.ts` `buildReportStrip(metrics, periodLabel)` →
  `StripTile[]` for the shared `KpiStrip` (✦ Explain on every tile, as before).
  Deltas are the payload's `change_pct` (gross revenue, orders, AOV) with the
  comparison in the sub-line ("vs prior day" / "vs prior 7 days" / "vs prior 30
  days"); Net revenue's sub is "after refunds"; Product margin's is "on N% of revenue",
  or **"Pending · fill cost-per-item in Shopify"** with a "—" value when the margin is
  null (SPEC-034: visibly pending, never a fake zero); Refunds shows the % of revenue
  (or the total when the % is absent) over "N refunds · $X". The strip is absent when
  the payload has no `sales` (as the tiles were).
- **Tables** (store performance, unfulfilled, same-store) use the Dashboard's compact
  head/cell classes (10px uppercase heads, 12px cells, `py-1.5`); lists (top products,
  channels, top styles, dead stock's top five) are hairline rows at `py-1.5` / 13px.
  Unfulfilled's 48h+ column keeps the destructive emphasis and every count keeps its
  drill-down.
- **Aged dead stock**: the three buckets as one row of three compact cells (the
  value alone — "tied up" is in the panel's header), the top five under them.
- **Risks · Opportunities** share a row with Sales by channel at ≥ lg; **Recommended
  actions** closes the report at full width with its primary tint. Under lg every
  grid stacks.
- `app/(app)/reports/loading.tsx` matches the new shape (a strip, then panels).

## Acceptance

1. The daily report at 1440px is materially shorter with the same data and every
   section present — measured with the live store: **2,462px → 1,582px (−36%)**.
2. Every figure on the old page appears on the new one; every drill-down count and
   location link still opens `/orders?…` in a new tab; PDF and Regenerate work.
3. `buildReportStrip` is Vitest-covered: six tiles; deltas and subs per period; the
   pending margin; refunds with and without `pct_of_revenue`; no `sales` → `[]`.
4. Weekly/monthly rollups render through the same layout with their note.
5. Docs: `lib/docs/content/reports.ts` describes the strip and the panel layout;
   `updated` bumped; screens recaptured. `npm test`, `tsc`, lint, build green.
